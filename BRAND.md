# Foundkeep Brand

**Name:** Foundkeep  
**Domain:** `foundkeep.app`  
**Line:** Found it? Keep it.  
**Promise:** Keep what matters with its original source.

Foundkeep is practical, curious, calm, and dependable. The name should always appear as one word with an uppercase F. Use “finds” for saved material in expressive copy and “captures” where precision matters.

The mark combines a warm-paper bookmark, a folded corner, and a vermilion origin point inside a near-black frame. The source SVGs live in `apps/extension/assets/mark.svg` and `apps/web/assets/mark.svg`; PNG exports are generated with `bun run brand:assets`.

The identity board is at `docs/brand/foundkeep-brand-board.png`. It defines visual direction, while the checked-in SVG remains the production source of truth.

Primary colors:

- Paper `#f3f3f0`
- Ink `#171917`
- Vermilion `#c63b23`
- Moss `#6b7553`

Use Clarity City for display and Geist for interface text. Keep the system flat, tactile, and editorial. Avoid gradients, mystical symbols, generic AI imagery, and decorative effects that weaken the source-and-bookmark metaphor.
