Welcome to FoundKeep, {{name}}

FoundKeep is a private place to keep the things worth keeping — links,
highlights, photos, files, even whole tweet threads — and actually find
them again later.

Three things to try:

1. Save from anywhere — add the browser extension or share into FoundKeep
   from your phone. One tap and it's kept.
2. Let AI do the filing — every save gets a summary, tags and a category
   automatically, so it's searchable without you organizing a thing.
3. Find it again — search across everything, or follow the links between
   related saves.
