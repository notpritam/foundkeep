# Floating dock + deep purple: iPhone beta checklist

Foundkeep **1.0.0 (15)** is available to Foundkeep Internal. Open TestFlight → Foundkeep → Update. Build 15 adds a native glass module, so install this binary before testing; build 14 cannot acquire it through an OTA update. Use the already accepted internal tester account. The current release state is in [README.md](README.md).

1. Sign in with your existing Foundkeep account. Gallery should use purple accents, image previews and the floating Gallery/You dock with a separate plus button.
2. Scroll down through a populated collection. The large header controls move away and dock labels contract to icons. Scroll up to reveal the controls and labels. Search and quick-add remain in the compact top bar.
3. Tap Gallery and You, then the floating plus. Create a note with a folder and tags. Confirm it appears in Gallery. The dock should stay out of the way while typing and should not cover the editor or saved-item view.
4. Open a saved item. Try Edit/Organize, Open source and More in the header. Scroll to related saves and open one; confirm its explanation and original source make sense.
5. Scroll Gallery and Settings to the end. Every final item and action should move above the dock. Try dark mode, larger text, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency. Larger text keeps stacked dock labels visible.
6. In Safari, share an article to Foundkeep, choose a folder and tags, and save. Open Gallery and verify content, preview and source. Also share an image/PDF and retry a share after reconnecting from offline.

Native Simulator checks cover the dock, navigation, keyboard, both appearances and large text; real-phone gesture feel still benefits from your beta pass. Social-provider activation, APNs provider activation and a persistent offline collection are separate outstanding work. The temporary UI verification account is not a review login.
