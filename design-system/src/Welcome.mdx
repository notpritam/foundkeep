# FoundKeep design system

The approved design, live. Every story renders the product's own source —
`theme.css`, `review.css`, `review.html`, `picker.js`, `card-icons.js`, the
icons in `dock.js` — so what you see is what ships, and nothing is a
screenshot.

- **Foundations** — colours (light and dark tokens), type, corner radii and
  the icon family, measured from the real components.
- **Extension / Add details card** — the card that opens from the dock after a
  save, in each of its states: pick a folder, add and remove tags, share to a
  collection.
- **Extension / Card controls** — its pills, tag chips, title and note,
  buttons and the list picker, each with an *All states · light & dark* view.

The toolbar's **Theme** switches light and dark as the extension's appearance
setting does, **Viewport** sizes the canvas (the 380px card width to desktop),
and the pseudo-state button forces hover, focus or pressed on any story.
